# 🚛 Empty Miles Marketplace

### Turning Empty Truck Journeys into Profitable Opportunities

## 📌 About the Project

Empty Miles Marketplace is a full-stack logistics platform designed to reduce the problem of empty truck journeys in the transportation industry.

Every day, thousands of trucks transport goods from one location to another and often return without carrying any cargo. These empty return journeys, commonly known as *empty miles* or *deadhead miles*, result in wasted fuel, unnecessary transportation costs, reduced carrier profitability, and avoidable carbon emissions.

Our platform aims to address this inefficiency by connecting **shippers who need to transport goods with truck carriers who have unused capacity along their existing routes**.

Instead of allowing trucks to travel empty, the platform identifies suitable shipment opportunities that align with their routes, schedules, and available capacity. This creates a mutually beneficial system where shippers can access potentially lower transportation rates while carriers generate additional revenue from otherwise unused truck space.

## 🎯 Why Are We Building This?

Empty truck journeys represent a significant inefficiency in the logistics and transportation industry.

The problem affects both sides of the supply chain:

- **For truck carriers:** Returning without cargo means spending money on fuel, maintenance, and driver time without generating additional transportation revenue.
- **For shippers:** Finding affordable and reliable transportation can be difficult, especially when suitable trucks are already travelling along nearby routes.
- **For the environment:** Unnecessary empty journeys consume fuel and contribute to greenhouse gas emissions.

Traditional logistics processes often rely on manual coordination, brokers, phone calls, and fragmented communication. This makes it difficult to efficiently connect available truck capacity with nearby shipment requirements.

We are building Empty Miles Marketplace to make this process more organized, accessible, and efficient through technology.

## 💡 Our Proposed Solution

Empty Miles Marketplace introduces a two-sided digital marketplace where:

- Truck carriers can list their upcoming empty or partially occupied journeys, including routes, schedules, vehicle types, and available capacity.
- Shippers can publish transportation requirements by specifying pickup and delivery locations, cargo details, weight, and delivery time windows.
- An intelligent route-matching system identifies compatible trucks and shipments based on route suitability, available capacity, timing, and additional travel distance.
- A dynamic pricing mechanism calculates suitable transportation prices, helping shippers save money while allowing carriers to earn additional revenue.
- A carbon savings estimator provides insights into the potential environmental benefits of utilizing existing truck capacity.

The goal is to transform otherwise unproductive truck journeys into economically valuable transportation opportunities.

## 🌱 Our Vision

We envision a more connected and efficient logistics ecosystem where unused transportation capacity can be discovered and utilized instead of being wasted.

Through Empty Miles Marketplace, we aim to:

1. Reduce unnecessary empty truck journeys.
2. Help carriers improve vehicle utilization and generate additional revenue.
3. Provide shippers with more affordable transportation opportunities.
4. Improve the efficiency of shipment discovery and route-based matching.
5. Promote more sustainable transportation practices by estimating avoidable emissions.

## 🚀 The Bigger Picture

Empty Miles Marketplace is more than just a platform for finding available trucks. It represents an approach towards smarter resource utilization in the logistics industry.

By combining digital marketplaces, route-based matching, transparent pricing, and environmental impact estimation, we aim to make transportation more cost-efficient, accessible, and sustainable.

**Our idea is simple: If a truck is already travelling somewhere, why should it travel empty when it could be carrying someone's shipment?**
